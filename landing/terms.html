What Dry Run is

A free practice drill for digital arrest scam calls. The officer, the agency and the case in every drill are fictional.

What it is not

It is not legal, financial or security advice, and it is not a substitute for official guidance from a bank, a police service or a government helpline.

No guarantee

Practising a call does not prevent fraud. Use the drill as one small rehearsal, not as a promise of safety.

Your session

You can delete your session and its debrief at any time. Sessions are removed automatically after seven days.

Fair use

Use the drill for yourself and your family. Do not use it to impersonate an agency or to help anyone defraud.
