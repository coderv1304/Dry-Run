What this is and is not

What it is

  • A practice drill for spotting a digital arrest scam call
  • A short, kind debrief written from the rules of the drill, not from a guess
  • Free, with no account, no payment and no microphone needed

What it is not

  • Not protection from fraud, and not a measure of your risk
  • Not a test of you. There is no pass and no fail
  • Not official advice from any agency, bank or telecom

Nothing real is needed

  • No real details

    Never share an OTP, card number, PIN or account detail. The drill warns you when a message looks like personal data.

  • Stop any time

    End drill stays on screen for the whole call, and chat stays available if the microphone is not.

  • Delete when you like

    Delete the transcript and the debrief from the debrief screen. Anything left is removed after seven days.

Questions people ask

What is a digital arrest scam?

Someone claims to be from the police, the CBI, the ED or customs and says you are under digital arrest. No agency asks you to stay on a video call or to move money to prove your innocence.

Is any of this real?

No. The officer, the agency and the case are fictional. The drill exists so you can hear the pressure once without any risk.

Do I need to give a phone number or an email address?

No. There is no account. A drill needs only the link you opened, and the browser tab can be closed at any time.

What does the drill keep?

Your messages and the debrief, so the debrief can be shown back to you. Anything left is removed after seven days, and you can delete the session from the debrief screen.

Will this stop a scammer?

No. Practice is not protection. It may help you notice the pressure sooner and reach the habit that ends the call.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no price, no plan and nothing to buy.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The drill runs in a browser and in the Android app. If the microphone is unavailable, the chat still works.

What if I type something real, like an OTP?

The drill flags messages that look like personal data and reminds you not to share them. In a real call, never share an OTP, a card number, a PIN or an account detail.

Can I take the drill with my family?

Yes. Create a private link and send it to one person. The link expires on the date shown when you create it, and the debrief can be shared with a report link.

Who made this?

Dry Run is a small practice project. It is not run by any agency, bank or telecom.
